The Grand Online Casino

WASD / arrows to move, Shift to run, Space to jump, mouse to look.
Press E at a slot machine to spin. Use + / - to raise or lower your bet. At the plinko board press E to drop a ball. At blackjack you sit in the dealer's chair: E deals, H hits, S stands, and Q leaves. At the roulette and dice tables press E to sit; at dice, hold left click on the dice and release to throw.
The door on the left wall leads to the Dark Room: press E near an addict to rob them (40%, once per 2 min each), but beware their punches. Buy a bat (1000 credits) at the shop on the right and left click to knock them out; robbing a body has an 80% chance. The axe (2500) chops off the limb or head you hit and kills the addict; pick up limbs with E and sell them to the mysterious man in the corner of the Dark Room (arms/legs 20, heads 35). Dead addicts respawn after 2 minutes. The flashlight, bat and axe live in the hotbar (1, 7, 6); R cycles tools and F toggles the flashlight when held. Keep the depression meter full for two minutes and you lose your sanity.
The pill dealer next to the weapons shop sells consumables; use hotbar keys 2-5 to take them. Losing bets or getting knocked out fills the depression meter; antidepressants lower it.
Esc to return to the menu.
